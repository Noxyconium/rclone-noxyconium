Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Rclone Noxyconium is a private synchronization utility operated solely by its owner.

Data use

The utility accesses Google Drive only to perform file listing, upload, download, update, and deletion operations explicitly authorized by the owner. It does not collect, sell, share, or use Google user data for advertising or analytics.

Data storage

Files are transferred directly between the owner's private computer and Google Drive. OAuth credentials and configuration are stored only on the owner's private system and are not transmitted to any third-party application server.

Access and revocation

The owner may revoke the utility's Google Drive access at any time from their Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions can be submitted to the owner through the project's GitHub repository.
